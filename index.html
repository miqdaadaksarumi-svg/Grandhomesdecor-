About the Studio

A beautiful home should also work hard for the people living in it.

Grandhomes Atelier begins every project with how a family actually moves through their space, before a single material is chosen. From full home transformations to focused single-room refreshes, we bring together considered materials, functional layouts, and a restrained, elevated palette — tailored to each client, never templated.
