Spec Sheet

How a project comes together

01
Discover
We begin by understanding your space, your lifestyle, your taste, and how you want the finished space to feel and function. Whether it's a home, office, hospitality space, or commercial project, we take the time to understand what you need before anything is selected or designed.

Through an in-person consultation or remotely via WhatsApp, we assess your space, requirements, measurements, preferences, and budget, giving us the foundation to create a space that is truly yours.
02
Design
This is where your ideas become a complete design concept.

We develop the interior direction of your space, from space planning and furniture selection to materials, colours, lighting, mosaic tiles, wall finishes, rugs, and decorative accessories.

Every element is carefully considered and curated to work together, whether we are designing a single room or an entire property. Our furniture and decor pieces can be sourced and customised specifically for your project, while our curated mosaic tiles and decorative accessories add the details that bring the design together.

The result is a cohesive design concept created specifically around your space, lifestyle, taste, and budget.
03
Execute
Once the design is approved, we take it from concept to reality.

We handle the sourcing and procurement of furniture, mosaic tiles, decorative accessories, and other materials required for your project. We also coordinate production, custom pieces, delivery, installation, styling, and the finishing details that complete the space.

From the first design decision to the final decorative piece, we manage the process so you don't have to chase multiple vendors or figure everything out yourself.

One vision. One process. One team, from design to finished space.
